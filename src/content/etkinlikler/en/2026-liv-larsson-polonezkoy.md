To help you widen your Nonviolent Communication lens, CNVC Certified Trainer, mediator and author Liv Larsson is coming to Turkey in October. In this residential training of three nights in Polonezköy, Istanbul:

- You can take up feelings as dynamic structures, and see how even observation is shaped by the mind.
- You can deepen your capacity for empathic listening by discovering how meaning is created together in every interaction.
- We will look at ways of increasing our capacity to hold complexity, and so to understand several perspectives at once and see their subtleties.
- You can grasp how multiple dimensions — inner, outer, relational and systemic — create every moment of life together.

Prerequisite: having completed at least a year-long training programme.
