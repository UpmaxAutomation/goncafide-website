What can you do when you choose your words with care and still go unheard?

Instead of telling the person in front of you "I understand what you said", what else might you say?

What would you change in your communication habits to be heard, and to hear?

If you are curious about the causes of communication accidents — and their antidote — this online workshop is for you.
