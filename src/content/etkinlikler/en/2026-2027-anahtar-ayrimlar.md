You are invited to the Key Differentiations Programme, enriched in its eighth year with new material and new practices.

Participants have said again and again that this programme eased their journey into Nonviolent Communication. I have enriched it further with Prof. Dr. Lisa Feldman Barrett's approach to emotion and with Prof. Dr. Türker Kılıç's Science of Connectivity and Culture of Living Together.

Across 33 online meetings and 82.5 hours we look together at 54 key differentiations, and experience where they serve as guides in life.

Those who have completed at least 36 hours of Nonviolent Communication training can join, to make using the language a practice and to grasp its subtleties.
