## About the programme

The Conflict Transformation Programme is designed for advanced participants who want to transform conflict and disagreement through NVC-based approaches. It aims both at developing capacity within your own conflicts and at being able to accompany others through theirs.

NVC-based mediation, third-party empathy, conflict in group dynamics, and systemic disagreements are the programme's main territory.

## Who is it for?

This programme suits those with at least one to two years of NVC practice. It may be particularly valuable for people working in these areas:

- Managers and team leaders
- Human resources professionals
- Teachers, educators and facilitators
- Therapists, coaches and counsellors
- Community organisers and activists
- NVC practitioners and (prospective) trainers

## What the programme covers

### Module 1 — The anatomy of disagreement

You examine how conflict begins, how it escalates, and which unmet needs feed it. You begin to see conflict as information rather than as threat.

- Conflict cycles and triggers
- Mechanisms of defence and attack
- The unmet needs beneath the disagreement

### Module 2 — Working with your own conflict

Before you can help others, you have to work with your own reactivity. In this module:

- Working with being triggered, and with reactivity
- "Emergency empathy" — the practice of self-empathy
- Sitting with mourning and anger in difficult relationships

### Module 3 — NVC-based mediation

Step by step, the skills needed to mediate between two people or two groups:

- Preparation and creating the space beforehand
- Making sure both sides are heard
- Building the bridge from needs to solutions
- Agreements and next steps

### Module 4 — Group and systemic disagreements

Complex conflicts involving several people, or systemic power dynamics:

- Awareness of power, privilege and structural inequality
- The culture of conflict inside organisations
- Disagreements in communities and in public life

### Module 5 — Practice and supervision

Working with real situations, case presentations and group supervision:

- Participants bring their own cases
- Live role play and feedback
- Building a personal development plan

## Structure

- **Length:** Roughly 4 months
- **Sessions:** 12
- **Format:** Online and in person
- **Group size:** 6–8 people, for a small group and intensive practice
- **Also:** Practice-partner work in pairs between sessions
