## About the programme

*Anda Kal Canda Kal* is an introductory programme for anyone taking their first step into Nonviolent Communication. The Turkish name holds two invitations at the heart of NVC: stay in the present moment (*anda kal*) and stay connected to life (*canda kal*).

Across 36 hours you learn the core skills of Nonviolent Communication through both theory and a great deal of practice, in a supportive group. The programme runs online or in person.

## Who is it for?

Anyone can join — no prior knowledge of NVC is needed. If any of these sound like you, this programme was built for you:

- You want to communicate better in your relationships, at home, at work or among friends
- You are looking for a different approach to conflict and disagreement
- You want to express your own feelings and needs more clearly
- You want to put empathy into genuine practice
- You have heard of Nonviolent Communication, you are curious, and you do not know where to begin

## What the programme covers

Step by step, you explore and practise these core skills:

- **The four steps: observation, feeling, need, request** — the basic framework of NVC
- **What blocks communication** — noticing the habits that cut connection
- **The capacity for empathy** — meeting yourself and others with empathy
- **The capacity for honest expression** — clear, sincere, without judgment
- **Ways of saying "no" and "yes"** — boundaries and consent
- **Building common ground** — finding solutions from shared needs

…and more. Every topic is grounded in real-life examples and live practice.

## Structure

- **Length:** 36 hours
- **Format:** Online or in person
- **How you learn:** Concepts, plenty of live practice, and group sharing
- **Where:** Zoom (online) or the agreed venue (in person)

## What you will take away

By the end of the programme you will:

- Recognise and apply the four steps — observation, feeling, need, request
- Be able to shift into NVC language in everyday conversation
- Connect more deeply, with yourself and with others, through empathy
- Have a concrete set of tools to reach for in moments of conflict

## Where to next

Those who complete this programme can move on to the **Key Differentiations Programme** to deepen their practice.
