## About the programme

Built to bridge the gap between learning NVC and *living* it, this programme is for participants who have completed an introductory training. It concentrates on the practice of turning conceptual knowledge into something that works in the real situations of daily life.

It follows naturally from the *Attunement into Now* introductory programme, though those who took their introduction with another trainer or programme are equally welcome.

## Who is it for?

- Those who have completed *Attunement into Now*
- Those who have taken another introductory NVC training
- Those familiar with NVC concepts who find them hard to apply

**Prerequisite:** Knowing the four components of NVC — observation, feeling, need, request.

## What the programme covers

### The shape of each session

Every session begins with real-life situations. Participants bring a moment from the past week they found hard, a friction in a relationship, or a situation they have not yet dared try. The group works with that material together.

**Skill focuses:**

1. **Slowing down at the moment of being triggered** — how do you respond rather than react?
2. **Being with yourself through empathy** — the inner practice of NVC
3. **Starting difficult conversations** — from where, and how?
4. **Hearing "no" and saying it** — boundaries and needs
5. **Repair after conflict** — rebuilding the relationship
6. **Integration and continuing** — what comes next?

### Structure

- **Length:** 6 weeks
- **Session length:** 2.5 hours
- **Group size:** 6–10 people
- **Where:** Zoom
- **Format:** Mostly practice, little presentation
