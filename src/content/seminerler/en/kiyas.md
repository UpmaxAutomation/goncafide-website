Comparing is a habit most of us know well. The envy and jealousy that surface when
we measure ourselves against others are often covered over with shame, and left
unspoken.

In this single-session workshop we take up these feelings without judgment, through
the needs-based approach of NVC. The aim is not to suppress them, but to hear the
needs they point to, and to build a more compassionate relationship with ourselves.
