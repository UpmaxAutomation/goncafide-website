Withdrawing into silence is usually not anger but a hurt that went unheard. It is a
protection that steps in where words have failed — and, at the same time, an attempt
to reach for connection.

In this workshop we take up that silence without judgment: what is our silence
trying to say? Which of our needs went unmet? In the language of NVC, we try out
ways of expressing ourselves that keep connection rather than withdraw from it.
