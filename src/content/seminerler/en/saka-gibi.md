Laughter connects — and sometimes it wounds. The same joke can put one person at
ease and hurt another.

In this workshop we take humour up as a question worth being curious about: what are
we laughing at? Which of our needs does that laughter touch? When does humour build
connection, and when does it create distance? Through the observation–feeling–need
lens of NVC, we look at humour with a compassionate awareness.
