Beneath most conflict lies a simple longing: to be heard. "Hear Me" is an
introduction to the experience of being heard, and of being able to hear.

In this session we meet what listening with empathy actually is, and how we can
express ourselves without blaming ourselves or the person in front of us, through
the basic steps of NVC. A warm beginning for anyone taking their first step into
Nonviolent Communication.
