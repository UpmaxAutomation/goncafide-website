When we say "may the evil eye not touch it", what exactly are we anxious to protect?
The unease that arrives alongside pleasure when we are admired; the fine line between
envying and being envied…

In this thematic workshop we take up these feelings — which run deep in our culture —
with an unjudging curiosity. Through the needs-based approach of NVC, we notice our
needs for being seen, for appreciation, for trust and for safety, and let connection
grow stronger.
